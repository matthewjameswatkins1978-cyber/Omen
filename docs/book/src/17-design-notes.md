# 17. Why Omen is designed this way

## Why no OmenScript?

Because real languages already exist.

Omen needs bounded composition, not another ecosystem of loops, modules, packages and debugging tools.

## Why is @last personal?

Because another actor's unrelated operation should not steal your conversational reference.

Shared machine reality and personal interaction history are different things.

## Why is DIRTY not failure?

Because evidence can stop being current without becoming historically false.

## Why can Omen say UNKNOWN?

Because uncertainty is real state.

A truthful UNKNOWN is more useful than an inferred green tick.

## Why semantic providers instead of more grep?

Because text occurrence, syntax structure and symbol identity are different questions.

## Why explicit AI invocation?

Because AI judgement is useful but qualitatively different from deterministic state.

## Why separate capability from authority?

Because "this machine knows how" and "this actor may" are different propositions.

## Why bounded output?

Because human attention and model context are scarce resources.

## Why contract digest and context generation are separate?

Because the meaning of semantic.definition should not change every time a file changes.

Static contract can be cached. Live environment evolves.

## Why is the prompt quiet?

Because persistent UI must earn its space.

## Why cross-platform by default?

Because agents are especially vulnerable to accidental platform assumptions.

## Why evidence?

Because confident prose decays.

Executable proof is harder to fool.
